## Initial AI output

Captured from Gemini in the app after sending the sample prompt:

> Here are three practical, high-impact tips to help you prepare effectively
> for your exams:
>
> 1. **Use Active Recall Instead of Passive Reading**
>
>    Don't just re-read your notes or highlight textbooks. Test yourself! Use
>    flashcards, answer practice questions, or explain a concept out loud to a
>    friend without looking at your notes. Forcing your brain to retrieve
>    information strengthens your memory far more than passive reading.
>
> 2. **Space Out Your Prep (Avoid Cramming)**
>
>    Break your study material into smaller chunks and review them over several
>    days or weeks. For example, studying a subject for 45 minutes a day over
>    five days is much more effective than a single 4-hour cram session. Try
>    the **Pomodoro Technique** (25 minutes of focused study followed by a
>    5-minute break) to maintain focus.
>
> 3. **Simulate the Exam Environment**
>
>    Practice with past papers or mock questions under actual exam
>    conditions—set a timer, put away your notes, and sit in a quiet space.
>    This helps reduce test anxiety, builds stamina, and teaches you how to
>    pace your time properly during the real exam.
>
> Good luck with your studying! Let me know if you need help creating a study
> schedule or trying out specific revision techniques.
